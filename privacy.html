What this covers

This policy describes how O&H Services ("we", "us") handles data connected through software integrations, including our internal QuickBooks Online integration ("the App").

What data the App accesses

The App is a private, internal tool operated solely by O&H Services staff. It connects to our own QuickBooks Online company file using OAuth authorization granted by our own administrator, and reads only the accounting records needed for internal bookkeeping: customers, invoices, bills, payments, deposits, accounts, items, and journal entries.

How the data is used and stored

Data accessed by the App is used only to produce internal work logs and invoices for our own business. Credentials and accessed data are stored on private infrastructure controlled by O&H Services. We do not sell, rent, share, or publish QuickBooks data with any third party, and we do not use customer data to train AI or machine-learning models.

Data retention and deletion

The App retains only what appears in our internal records. Access can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the integration in QuickBooks Online, after which no further data is accessed. Historical records are deleted on request by contacting us below.
